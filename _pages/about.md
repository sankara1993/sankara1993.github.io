Hey there, and welcome to my little corner of the internet! I'm so glad you stopped by. Pull up a chair and stay a while. Let me start by telling you a little about how I got here.

It began in India, where I was born, and then the Bay Area of California, where I grew up. I've always been driven by curiosity and a love of adventure. Growing up around so many cultures and opportunities gave me a taste for exploring new places and taking on new challenges. I went to the University of California, Irvine, where I split my time between studying and soaking up sunny SoCal life. After graduating, I settled in San Diego for a few years, enjoying the coastal vibes and staying active and on the move.

Travel has always been a big part of my story, from solo trips across different countries to spontaneous road trips. During the pandemic, I took the unconventional route and drove across the country while working remotely. It gave me a fresh perspective on life and a deeper appreciation for the beauty and variety of the U.S.

In 2022, I moved to Chicago with my girlfriend at the time. A year later, just before we relocated to Columbus, Ohio, I proposed, and we got married in 2024! These days we're enjoying married life and getting to know a new city together.

Today I work remotely as an Analytics Manager at Thermo Fisher Scientific, and I'm pursuing my MBA at The Ohio State University's Fisher College of Business, with graduation planned for 2027. I love to keep learning and growing, whether that's digging deeper into data and tech, discovering what makes Columbus special, or chasing fitness goals. Running has become a big part of that. In October 2026 I ran my third Columbus Marathon, and I'm already thinking about what comes next. We're planning to stay in Ohio until around 2027, and after that, who knows where life will take us?

This blog is where I share the journey: passion projects, personal reflections, travel stories, race recaps, and maybe a few tips for balancing a busy life. I hope you find something here that resonates with you, or that simply makes you smile.

Thanks so much for visiting. I'd love to hear from you, so please say hello anytime!
