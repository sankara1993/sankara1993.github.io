Hey there! Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

I was born in India, raised in the vibrant Bay Area of California, and have always been fueled by a sense of adventure and curiosity. Growing up surrounded by diverse cultures and endless opportunities, I developed a love for exploring new places and taking on new challenges. I attended the University of California, Irvine, where I balanced studying and enjoying sunny SoCal life. After graduation, I settled in San Diego for a while, soaking up the coastal vibes and continuing to cultivate my passion for staying active and traveling.

Traveling has been a core part of my life — whether it's solo trips across different countries or spontaneous road trips. During the pandemic, I took the unconventional route and drove across the country while working remotely. This experience not only gave me a fresh perspective on life but also deepened my appreciation for the beauty and diversity of the U.S.

In 2022, life took an exciting turn when I moved to Chicago with my then-girlfriend. A year later, just before relocating to Columbus, Ohio, I proposed, and we tied the knot in 2024! Now, we're enjoying married life while exploring a new city together.

Currently, I'm pursuing an MBA at The Ohio State University and working full-time remotely for Thermo Fisher Scientific. I'm constantly learning and growing — both professionally and personally — whether it's by diving deeper into tech, taking on mini fitness challenges, or immersing myself in Columbus' unique culture. We're planning to stay in Ohio until 2027, but after that, who knows where life will take us?

This blog is my way of sharing the journey — from passion projects to personal reflections, travel stories, and maybe even a few tips on balancing a busy life. I hope you find something here that resonates with you or simply makes you smile.

Thanks for stopping by, and I'm looking forward to connecting!
