Every run I've logged on Strava since 2019, turned into an interactive dashboard: 711 runs, 3,922 miles, and 544 hours moving, broken down by year, pace, shoe, and workout type.

The dashboard is built entirely from my own Strava data export (no third-party API), including GPS tracks parsed straight from the raw files. It includes a world map of the 29 places I've logged a GPS-tracked run, a route heatmap for my most-run cities, and filters to slice everything by year.
